Hi there! I am a Forward Deployed and Artificial Intelligence Engineer at Ascend Partners. I recently graduated with a B.S. in Applied Mathematics, a B.A. in Computer Science, and a minor in Music from the University of Rochester. My research interests lie at the intersection of mathematics and computer science—with current emphasis on augmented reality and robotics.

In addition to my studies, I played DIII college football and was a piano student at the Eastman School of Music. In the past, I've played in Carnegie Hall and have performed with a variety of chamber ensembles. Music has been a large source of inspiration for my work in tech.

If I'm not hitting the books you can find me at the piano, playing tennis, or outside on a drive.

Music

I started playing the piano when I was 8 years old and began composing at 18. During my childhood I competed intensely, receiving 1st place at the USIDPC and Judge's Award at American Protégé, and subsequently made my debut at Carnegie Hall.

In college, I auditioned and joined the Eastman School of Music as a piano student; it was a highlight of my time in Rochester. The cold bus to the conservatory and late-night practice sessions in the Chapel are some of my most cherished memories.

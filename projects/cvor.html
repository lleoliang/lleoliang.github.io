Cross-View Object Registration (CVOR)

C++ · Python · Computer Vision · Augmented Reality

An augmented reality system that lets you see through walls—it takes object detections from a remote camera and overlays them as spatially registered objects in the wearer's view.

Presented at the University of Rochester's 2026 XR Research Symposium

Description

My most ambitious project to date, inspired by a need for better situational awareness in mission-critical scenarios such as search and rescue operations, emergency services, and military applications.

In building CVOR with no background in robotics, I humoursly ended up re-deriving several foundational concepts in the field from scratch. I deeply enjoyed the process as it allowed me to see the direct application of mathematics and intuition to engineering principles.

I've now worked extensively with stereo cameras and AR headsets such as the Microsoft HoloLens2 and Snapchat Spectacles.

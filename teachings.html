Teaching is an integral part of my life. I have taught math for nine years and piano for seven.

Multivariable Calculus TA

I was a teaching assistant for MATH 164, Multivariable Calculus, at the University of Rochester for three years. I wrote workshop problems, challenge problems, and detailed worked solutions for over 200 students.

Using the materials

Nearly everything comes as a PDF with its LaTeX source. Answer keys and teacher notes are listed separately from the student copies. Some materials do not have answer keys. They should be understood by the teacher.

Sources use a deliberately minimal LaTeX preamble (geometry, amsmath, array, pgffor, tikz), so they compile anywhere with a standard distribution.

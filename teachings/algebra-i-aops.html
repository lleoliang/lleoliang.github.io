Algebra I Supplements

Private course · AoPS Introduction to Algebra

Problem sets and a quiz that supplement a private course built on the AoPS Introduction to Algebra textbook, keyed to chapters 14 and 15.

What's here

Homework 1 and the quiz cover graphing quadratics, including vertex form and completing the square. Homework 2 covers circles and quadratic and rational inequalities. They are meant to be used alongside the textbook and don't reproduce any of it.

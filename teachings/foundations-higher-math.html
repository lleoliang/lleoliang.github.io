Foundations of Higher Math

10-class summer camp · middle school

A ten-class summer camp that takes advanced middle-school students from math terminology through sets, functions, and coordinate geometry to an intuition-first introduction to pre-calculus.

The Design

Each class' content builds on the last. The vocabulary from the first class becomes the language of sets, sets lead to functions (including a look at how Georg Cantor compared infinite sets [you can imagine the students' confused faces lol]), and functions lead into rates of change and limits. Most classes come with a problem set, and the early classes also have guided notes and worksheets.

Materials are grouped by class below. Student copies are what I handed out; Key / teacher holds the answer keys and the teacher notes.

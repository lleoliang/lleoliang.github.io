MATH 164 · University of Rochester · Teaching assistant

Workshop problems, challenge problems, and worked solutions I wrote as a teaching assistant for Multivariable Calculus.

About these materials

These are some of the workshop problems, challenge problems, and detailed solution write-ups I created for students. The Stokes' theorem write-up, for example, fills in steps that the textbook skips.

The referred textbook is Calculus: Early Transcendentals 8th Edition by James Stewart, Daniel Clegg, and Saleem Watson. (If a section doesn't match, it is the 9th edition.)

Workshop problem: motion in space (September 2023)A fly on a spiral path, from Section 13.4: velocity, speed, and acceleration, whether it is speeding up or slowing down (using the dot product), where it crosses a plane, and whether a thrown rock hits it. Ends with a challenge problem.PDF
Workshop problem: tangent lines and planes (October 2023)A rocket that touches a sphere-shaped Earth at exactly one point. Find one such path as a tangent line, then describe every such path with the tangent plane.PDF
Challenge problem: gradients, double integrals, optimization (November 2023)A room with a curved ceiling: the area of a domain from a double integral, the highest point of the ceiling, the direction of steepest ascent, a directional derivative, and solving for a room dimension from a given volume.PDF

Final exam, Problem 7: solutionSetting up volume integrals in cylindrical and spherical coordinates, for a cylinder–sphere region and a cone–hemisphere region. Set up only, not evaluated.PDF
